# ADR-039 — Formulaic constructions as standalone findings

Status: accepted. The user asked why a review of copy written deliberately in an AI voice
reported nothing; the measurement below answered it, and this records the change that followed.

## Current design

[ADR-037](ADR-037.md) allows a deterministic signal to stand alone as a finding when it is
aggregate and objectively countable: three or more distinct promotional phrases in one node, or
three or more sentences sharing an opening word. It explicitly refuses standalone status to
single-phrase matches and to density heuristics, because those are where legitimate promotional
writing gets caught.

Every rule family is built on vocabulary — a list of low-information phrases, a list of padded
phrasings, superlative and adverb density — or on sentence statistics.

## Problem and consequence

A real pull request rewrote a product's landing page, footer and metadata "to read like typical
AI writing". Humanize reviewed it and published *Nothing to flag*. The deterministic layer
produced **zero signals across all twelve changed nodes**, and the model's candidates were all
suppressed, so nothing reached the author.

The copy was AI-styled in a way no rule could see, because its vocabulary is ordinary and
product-specific while its *architecture* is templated:

- `It's Not Just Music — It's Science You Can Feel`
- `Each soundscape is intentionally designed — not randomly generated — to support a specific mental state.`
- `Your journey begins here — no guesswork, just sound engineered for how the brain actually works.`
- `In a world of endless playlists and algorithm-driven noise, NeuroRhythms takes a different approach.`
- `Scientifically Engineered Sound — Designed for the Mind`

Not one of these contains a listed phrase. Defining a thing by what it is not, contrasting
against a strawman, setting a scene, and restating a heading after a dash are formulas, and the
product had no way to observe any of them. A content reviewer blind to this shape of writing is
blind to a large part of what it exists to review.

## Decision and alternatives

A **formulaic construction** may stand alone as a finding, alongside the aggregate signals
ADR-037 already permits. A construction is a matched sentence architecture, not a matched word:
the pattern must span the whole formula, and matching punctuation or a single common word is
never sufficient.

Five are recognised initially: negation reframing, strawman contrast, no-X-just-Y, scene-setting
openers, and em-dash tagline appositives. They are advisory, carry `minor` severity, are marked
deterministic, and pass through the ordinary noise budget like any other finding.

This extends rather than contradicts ADR-037. That decision excluded single *phrase* matches
because a phrase such as "cutting-edge" appears in honest writing as readily as in generated
writing. A construction is far narrower: `not just X — it's Y` is a template, and its presence is
as objectively checkable as counting repeated sentence openings.

Alternatives considered. Leaving these to the model was rejected because it is what already
happened: the model proposed candidates and the verifier discarded all of them, so observable,
checkable problems depended on model capacity. Emitting them as non-standalone evidence was
rejected for the same reason. Flagging em dashes or contrast by frequency was rejected outright —
they are ordinary tools of good writing, and the measurement below exists to keep it that way.

## Implementation impact

`CONSTRUCTIONS` in `packages/rules` emits these signals with `standalone: true`. No schema, wire
format or migration changes. Wording continues to describe the observable pattern and never
asserts how the text was produced (INV-006).

## Amendment, same day: corroboration required

Published as written, this decision let a single construction stand alone. Research on detection
is consistent that no single marker carries a judgement — the em dash was learned from
well-edited human prose, `not X, it's Y` predates the machines, and combined feature sets reach
F1 ≈ 0.94 where a single measure reaches ≈ 0.81. A construction is therefore evidence, and a
finding is published only where **two independent families** agree on one passage. Families are
counted, not occurrences, so one habit cannot corroborate itself. Recall fell 12/15 to 10/15;
precision stayed at 100%. The method is set out in
[ai-style-methodology.md](../evaluation/ai-style-methodology.md).

## Verification and approval evidence

Nine cases were added to the review gold set: the five constructions above, taken verbatim from
the pull request that went unreviewed, and four deliberately hard negatives that use the same
punctuation and contrast legitimately — an em-dash aside carrying real information, a specific
technical contrast, an em dash as a plain heading separator, and a correcting "not a failure".

`evals/review/deterministic.test.ts` measures the deterministic layer alone, with no model, and
runs in the default lane because a standalone rule answers to no verifier:

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| positives flagged | 7 / 15 | **12 / 15** |
| false positives | 0 / 20 | **0 / 20** |
| precision | 100% | **100%** |
| recall | 47% | **80%** |

Precision on the twenty do-not-comment cases, including all seven labelled legitimate, is the
release-blocking property here: a standalone finding reaches a pull request whether or not any
model agrees. The test names every case that wrongly fires rather than reporting a count.
