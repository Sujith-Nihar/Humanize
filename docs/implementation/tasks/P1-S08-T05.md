- Last updated: 2026-09-18T18:11:51.322241+00:00.
- Implementation revision: current working tree; no commit claimed.
- Evidence: Review gold set now holds 26 labelled cases: 16 clear-cut and 10 covering the ambiguous middle that was previously absent, namely mediocre human copy and edited AI-assisted copy. Ambiguous labels are marked as judgement calls and scored separately, because a failure there may mean the label is wrong rather than the reviewer. MEASURED with qwen3.5:4b: overall precision 100% recall 50%; clear-cut precision 100% recall 80%; AMBIGUOUS PRECISION 100% RECALL 20%. Precision held at 100% across all 26 cases, including every ambiguous negative: passive voice, an edited AI sentence, a long but information-dense sentence, a hedged claim and correct industry jargon were all left alone. The product does not nag. Recall on the ambiguous middle is 20%, with four of five missed: padded phrasing such as "provides users with the ability to", stacked redundancy such as "In order to get started, you will first need to begin by", residual AI wording such as "empowers teams to seamlessly streamline", and a vague tagline. Only the uniform three-clause cadence was caught, and by a deterministic rule rather than the model. TWO FINDINGS. First, the missed cases share a shape the product has no rule for: wordiness and padding, as distinct from promotional vocabulary. Second, the deterministic phrase list matches literal strings and not morphological variants, so "leveraging the power of" does not match the listed "leverage the power of"; that is why generic-docs-opener fell to two matched phrases, below the three-phrase cluster threshold, and was missed this run although the model flagged it in an earlier one. Clear-cut recall therefore sits exactly at the 0.8 floor and will flake with model variance. Results: pnpm check 187 unit tests across 31 files.
- Next action / remaining acceptance: Deliberately NOT tuned further in this session: chasing 20% ambiguous recall against 10 cases would overfit to the corpus rather than improve the product. The evidence-led order for whoever continues is: add a padding and wordiness rule family, since the misses share that shape and such phrases are as countable as the promotional ones; make phrase matching stem-aware so leverage, leverages and leveraging all match; enlarge the ambiguous set well beyond 10 cases before trusting any recall number from it; and re-measure with a larger local model to separate prompt and threshold behaviour from model capacity, since the same case flipped between runs at temperature 0. The specification gate still requires independent human judgement of usefulness, which no measurement here provides.
