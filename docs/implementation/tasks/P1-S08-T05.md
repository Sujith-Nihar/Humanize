Five formulaic constructions were added (negation reframing, strawman contrast, no-X-just-Y, scene-setting opener, em-dash tagline appositive) plus a punctuation family. Research on detection is consistent that no single marker carries a judgement, so a construction is evidence and a finding publishes only where TWO independent families agree on one passage (ADR-039 as amended). Families are counted rather than occurrences, so one habit cannot corroborate itself. MEASURED by evals/review/deterministic.test.ts in the default lane, with no model: before constructions 7/15 positives, uncorroborated 12/15, corroborated 10/15; false positives 0/20 throughout, including seven cases labelled legitimate and four hard negatives that use em dashes and contrast correctly. Corroboration cost five points of recall and bought the guarantee that no finding rests on a single marker. The method, its justification, its sources and its gaps are written up in docs/evaluation/ai-style-methodology.md.
